# Pricing, Billing & Refunds

Heliora Analytics pricing is transparent and billed in USD.

## Plans overview

### Free
- Price: $0 / month
- Up to 50,000 events / month
- 2 seats
- Core dashboards and funnels
- Email support
- No API access
- Export: last 30 days, max 10,000 rows

### Pro
- Price: $79 / month (or $790 / year — save ~17%)
- Up to 2,000,000 events / month
- Up to 10 seats
- Advanced retention cohorts and saved funnels
- Chat + email support (target < 4 business hours)
- REST API (60 req/min)
- Full CSV export
- Custom dashboard sharing links

### Business
- Price: $249 / month (or $2,490 / year)
- Up to 15,000,000 events / month
- Unlimited seats
- Everything in Pro
- SAML SSO
- Scheduled S3 exports
- Dedicated success manager (annual contracts)
- Higher API limits (300 req/min)
- Priority support (target < 1 business hour)

## What’s on Pro?

Pro includes advanced retention cohorts, saved funnels, REST API access, full CSV export, custom dashboard sharing links, up to 2M events/month, up to 10 seats, and chat support. Pro does **not** include SAML SSO or scheduled S3 exports (Business only).

## How billing works

Plans renew automatically on your billing anniversary. You can pay by credit card (Visa, Mastercard, Amex). Business annual contracts can also pay by invoice (Net 30) after credit approval.

Taxes may apply based on your billing country. Invoices are available under Billing → Invoices.

## Changing plans

You can upgrade anytime; the change is prorated for the remainder of the billing period. Downgrades take effect at the next renewal. To change plans: Billing → Change plan.

If you exceed your event quota, tracking continues but new events are queued; overage is billed at $0.00004 per event on Pro and $0.000025 on Business. Free plan stops ingesting new events until the next month or until you upgrade.

## Refunds

Refunds are available under these rules:

1. **Monthly plans:** You may request a full refund within **14 days** of the initial paid charge if you have not used more than 10% of your monthly event quota.
2. **Annual plans:** You may request a prorated refund within **30 days** of purchase. After 30 days, annual plans are non-refundable except where required by law.
3. **How to request:** Email billing@heliora-analytics.example with your workspace ID and reason. Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days to the original payment method.
4. **Chargebacks:** Please contact billing before filing a chargeback so we can resolve the issue faster.

We do **not** refund for unused seats mid-cycle on monthly plans after the 14-day window, or for overage charges already incurred.

## Canceling your subscription

Cancel anytime under Billing → Cancel subscription. Access continues until the end of the paid period. After cancellation, event data is retained for 30 days, then permanently deleted unless you reactivate.

## Free trial

New workspaces get a **14-day Pro trial** with no credit card required. At the end of the trial you are moved to Free unless you choose a paid plan.
